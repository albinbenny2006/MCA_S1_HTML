MY FAVOURITE PLACE

Munnar

Munnar is one of the most beautiful hill stations in Kerala.It is famous for its green tea plantations, mountains, waterfalls and pleasant climate.

Munnar is a wonderful place to enjoy nature and spend time with family and friends. The beautiful scenery and peaceful atmosphere make it my favourite place.
